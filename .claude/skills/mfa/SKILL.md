# Multi-Factor Authentication

TOTP-based MFA lives in `auth-server/src/lib/mfa/` (KEK, recovery-code HMAC, TOTP via `otplib`, QR rendering, Redis challenge store) plus the `MfaRegistry` resource group at `auth-server/src/lib/auth-db/mfa/`. The login handler intercepts users with a verified factor and returns an `mfa_required` discriminated-union variant instead of an authorization code; the client completes the flow at `POST /api/auth/mfa/verify`. Required env vars: `PRIVATE_MFA_SECRET_KEK`, `PRIVATE_MFA_RECOVERY_PEPPER` (both 32-byte base64).

The account-management UI lives in the auth-server `src/components/` (it is consumed only by the auth-server, not by external resource servers) and renders on the dedicated `/mfa` route: `src/components/Mfa/` owns `TotpSettingsCard` plus the TOTP dialogs (`TotpEnrollmentDialog`, `MfaRemoveFactorDialog`, `MfaRegenerateRecoveryCodesDialog`), `src/components/RecoveryCodesPanel/` owns `RecoveryCodesPanel`, and `src/components/Passkeys/` owns `PasskeysSettingsCard`/`PasskeysSettingsSection` (the WebAuthn browser ceremonies). `@schemavaults/auth-ui` retains the login challenge-flow components (`MfaChallengeForm`, `MfaFactorPicker`) used at `/auth/mfa`. The React state hook is `useMfa()` in `@schemavaults/auth-react-provider`.

## Organization MFA requirements

Organizations can require their members to use MFA (migration 00048). "Has MFA" means at least one verified `USER_MFA_FACTORS` row (TOTP or passkey), the login challenge's test. Only real `ORGANIZATION_MEMBERSHIP_ROLES` rows are subject to it: service accounts' and platform admins' virtual memberships are not, and system (hardcoded) organizations cannot require MFA.

- **Tables**: `ORGANIZATION_MFA_REQUIREMENTS` (one row per organization that requires MFA: `grace_period_days` 1–90, `required_since`) and `ORGANIZATION_MFA_GRACE_PERIODS` (one row per member without MFA: `reason` `requirement_enabled` | `mfa_removed`, a fixed `ends_at`, `notified_at`; cascades from the requirement, so turning it off ends every grace period). DB functions: `auth-server/src/lib/auth-db/organization-mfa-requirements/`; client-safe rules and view types: `src/lib/organization-mfa-requirements/{policy,types}.ts`; side effects (emails, enforcement): `organization-mfa-requirement-actions.ts` there.
- **API**: `GET|PUT /api/organizations/{organization_id}/mfa-requirement` (members read; owners and platform admins change it and see `members_without_mfa`; a caller who is a member must have MFA to turn it on, 409 `mfa_required`), `GET /api/me/organization-mfa-requirements` (the caller's status per organization, suspended ones included), `GET|POST /api/admin/organizations/enforce-mfa-requirements` (admin session or `CRON_SECRET`; daily Vercel cron / host crontab).
- **Joining** requires MFA while the requirement is on: invitation acceptance answers 403 and direct assignment 409, both `error: "mfa_required"` (`wouldJoiningViolateOrganizationMfaRequirement()`); the invitation stays pending.
- **Grace periods** start when the requirement is turned on (every member without MFA, in the PUT's transaction) and when a member loses their last factor (`startOrganizationMfaGracePeriodsForUser()` after TOTP / passkey removal and the admin MFA reset); enrolling a factor clears them (`clearOrganizationMfaGracePeriodsAfterEnrollment()` after TOTP / passkey enrollment and in the test enrollment seed). The enforcement job also starts any missing ones, deletes stale ones, and retries unsent emails. A new `grace_period_days` only applies to grace periods started afterwards.
- **Suspension and removal**: once `ends_at` passes without MFA, `listUserOrganizationMemberships()` leaves the membership out (tokens, route guards, resource-server role lookups and `/api/me/organizations` all stop seeing it; enabling MFA lifts it), and the enforcement job deletes the membership row. No exception for an organization's last owner: platform admins can re-add an owner.
- **Emails**: `sendOrganizationMfaRequiredEmail()` sends the mail server's `organization-mfa-required` template (props `organizationName`, `gracePeriodEndsAt` ISO, `enableMfaUrl`, `reason`, `organizationUrl`, `productName`); failures are captured and left unnotified for the job to retry.
- **UI**: `OrganizationMfaRequirementCard` on `/orgs/[organization_id]` (SSR-preloaded by `loadOrganizationMfaRequirementView()`) and `OrganizationMfaRequirementBanner` in the authenticated layout (`src/components/OrganizationMfaRequirement/`).
- **E2E**: `organizations/OrganizationMfaRequirementApi.cy.ts`; the test-only `POST /api/test/seed/expire-organization-mfa-grace-period` ends a member's grace period now.
